About Our School

Poyiloor Central LP School was established in 1928 under the Poyiloor Muslim Jamath. For almost a century, our school has been providing quality education to children from Classes 1 to 5.

Our dedicated teachers provide individual attention to every child, encouraging creativity, confidence and academic excellence.
