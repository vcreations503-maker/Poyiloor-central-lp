About our school

Poyiloor Central LP School was established in 1928 under the Poyiloor Muslim Jamath. For almost a century, it has taught the children of our community from class 1 to class 5.

Every child is known by name here. Our teachers give each student personal attention, in the classroom and outside it.

What your child will learn

Speaking English

Daily speaking practice so children are not afraid to talk in English.

Knowing AI

Children learn what AI is and how to use it safely and wisely.

Strong basics

Regular worksheets, reading cards and mock tests, including scholarship exam practice.

Programs and events

Celebrations, competitions and activities that build confidence.
